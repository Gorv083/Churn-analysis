# Churn-analysis
The Customer Subscription Churn Analysis project focuses on understanding customer behavior and identifying the key factors that contribute to subscription cancellations. The analysis examines customer subscription data, including plan type, tenure, monthly charges, escalations, and churn status, to identify patterns among customers who are likely to leave.

The objective of this analysis is to calculate the overall churn rate, compare churn across different subscription plans, analyze revenue at risk from churned customers, and identify customer segments with higher cancellation rates. By analyzing these factors, businesses can understand customer retention challenges and identify opportunities to reduce churn.
